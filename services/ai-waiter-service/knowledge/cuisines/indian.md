# Indian

## How an Indian meal works
Curries (butter chicken, korma, tikka masala, dal makhani, paneer) with naan, roti or rice (jeera rice, biryani),
plus raita or salad. Tandoori dishes are dry and come with bread.
when: naan, roti, curry, কারি, biryani, paneer, tandoori, তান্দুরি

## Schedule
- morning 06:00-11:30: lead paratha, dosa, idli, poori, chole, upma, breakfast
- lunch 11:30-16:00: lead thali, biryani, rice, curry, dal, combo
- afternoon 16:00-19:00: lead samosa, pakora, chaat, tea, snack, tikka
- dinner 19:00-06:00: lead curry, naan, tandoori, biryani, tikka, dal

## Needs a base
- masala, curry, makhani, korma, vindaloo, butter chicken, tikka masala, paneer, kofta, dal -> naan, rice, roti, paratha, jeera rice, kulcha
