# Juice, shakes and lassi

## Kinds of drinks
Juices (fresh fruit, often regular/special), milkshakes, lassi (yogurt drink), mojitos/lemonades (fizzy, minty).
These are different drinks — if the guest asks for a kind the menu doesn't have (e.g. lassi in a juice bar), say so
and offer the nearest kind; never list juices as "lassi". "মোজো" usually means mojito.
when: juice, জুস, shake, শেক, lassi, লাচ্ছি, লাসি, mojito, মোজো, মোজিটো, lemonade, লেমনেড
