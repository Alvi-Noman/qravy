# Fast food (burgers, fried chicken, pizza, wings, sandwiches)

## How a fast-food meal works
A main (burger, fried chicken, pizza, sandwich, set/combo box) with a side (fries, wedges, onion rings, nachos) and
a drink (soft drink, shake, lemonade). Combos and meat boxes already include sides — check the description.

## Sizes and pieces
Wings, fried chicken and fries come in pieces or sizes (6/10 pcs, regular/large). When the guest says a count that
isn't offered ("4 pieces"), say which counts there are and ask — don't repeat the same question word for word.
when: piece, pcs, পিস, size, সাইজ, large, regular, small, বড়, ছোট

## Light vs filling
Light: fries, onion rings, nachos, drinks. Filling: burgers, fried chicken, combos, pizza, meat boxes. Wings are
a snack but not "light".
when: light, হালকা, filling, hungry, ক্ষুধা, খিদে, পেট ভরে
