# Café (coffee, tea, light bites)

## How a café order works
A drink (coffee, tea, shake, juice) with a small bite (sandwich, pastry, wedges, cake). Hot vs iced matters — never
call an iced drink "hot".

## Coffee
Espresso is small and strong; americano is espresso with water; latte and cappuccino have milk (cappuccino more
foam); mocha has chocolate. Iced versions are cold.
when: coffee, কফি, latte, cappuccino, espresso, americano, mocha, লাটে, ক্যাপুচিনো

## Tea
Many cafés have several teas (milk tea, masala, lemon, green, herbal). "চা" alone may need "which one?".
when: tea, চা, cha, chai
