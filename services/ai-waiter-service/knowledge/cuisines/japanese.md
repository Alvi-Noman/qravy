# Japanese

## How the meal works
Sushi and sashimi (raw fish — ask about raw preferences), rolls (often by 4/8 pieces), ramen/udon (hot noodle
soups), katsu/teriyaki with rice, gyoza as a starter.
when: sushi, সুশি, ramen, রামেন, roll, teriyaki, katsu, gyoza
