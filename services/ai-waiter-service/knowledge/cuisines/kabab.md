# Kebab & grill

## How a kebab meal works
Kebabs, tikka, chaap, grills and charcoal chicken are eaten with naan or paratha (sometimes rice), salad and a sauce
or chutney, often with a cold drink or lassi. Offer bread with kebabs.
when: kabab, kebab, কাবাব, grill, গ্রিল, tikka, টিক্কা, chaap, চাপ, শিক, boti, বটি

## Spice
Beef shik, naga and "spicy" grills are hot; reshmi, malai and hariyali kebabs are mild.
when: spicy, mild, ঝাল, ঝাল কম
