# Thai

## How a Thai meal works
Soups (tom yum — hot & sour, tom kha — coconut), curries (green/red/massaman) with rice, stir-fries and noodles
(pad thai), salads (som tam). Green curry and tom yum are spicy; massaman and pad thai are milder.
when: thai, থাই, tom yum, curry, pad thai, spicy, ঝাল
