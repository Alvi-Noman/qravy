# Chinese (incl. Bangladeshi-style Chinese)

## How a Chinese meal works
A typical order: a soup to start (Thai soup, corn soup, hot & sour), then fried rice or chowmein with one or two
"dry" dishes (chilli chicken, chilli onion, cashew nut salad, masala chicken, beef/prawn dishes) and maybe vegetables
or a sizzler. Portions are often for 1:2 or 1:3 people — say how many it serves when the menu says.

## Rice and noodles
Fried rice (egg, chicken, mixed, special) and chowmein/noodles are the base — never call fried rice "ভাত" and never
mix up chowmein and noodles. Suggest a curry or dry dish with them.
when: rice, fried rice, chowmein, noodle, রাইস, ফ্রাইড রাইস, চাওমিন, নুডলস, ভাত

## Soups
Soups come first, often in sizes (1:1, 1:3). Thai soup is spicy-sour, corn soup is mild.
when: soup, স্যুপ, সুপ

## What's usually inside (allergies and diets)
"Special" fried rice / chowmein / soup / salad usually mixes chicken, prawn and/or beef; wontons usually have a meat
or prawn filling; spring rolls may contain chicken; set menus and fried rice usually include egg; "Thai" dishes often
use fish sauce; vegetable dishes often use oyster sauce. Never call such a dish vegetarian or free of an allergen.
when: allergy, allergic, vegetarian, vegan, prawn, shrimp, egg, nut, অ্যালার্জি, এলার্জি, নিরামিষ, ভেজ, চিংড়ি, ডিম, বাদাম

## Spicy
Chilli, Szechuan, naga and "dry" dishes are spicy; sweet & sour, cashew, corn soup, masala are milder.
when: spicy, mild, ঝাল, ঝাল কম, jhal
