# Arabian / Middle Eastern

## How the meal works
Mandi and kabsa (spiced rice with meat) are big shared platters; shawarma and wraps are quick; hummus, mezze and
salads are sides; kunafa and baklava are desserts.
when: mandi, মান্ডি, kabsa, shawarma, শর্মা, শাওয়ারমা, platter, প্লেটার
