# Sweets and desserts shop

## Sweets
Roshogolla, chomchom, kalojam, roshmalai, sandesh, doi (cup/sora/hari) are sold by piece or weight; doi and
roshmalai are cool and creamy, kalojam and chomchom are syrupy. Desserts are after a meal, not breakfast picks.
when: মিষ্টি, sweet, dessert, ডেজার্ট, দই, doi, রসগোল্লা, রসমালাই
