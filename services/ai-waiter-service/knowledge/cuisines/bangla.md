# Bangladeshi / Bengali cuisine

What an experienced waiter in a Bangladeshi restaurant knows. Shared by every Bangladeshi restaurant — never a
specific restaurant's dish names here (those come from its own menu).

## How a Bangla meal works
A bhaat meal is rice with fish, meat or chicken curry, plus small sides: bhorta (mashed — aloo, begun, shutki, dim,
chingri, ilish…), bhaji (fried/stir-fried vegetables — begun bhaja, alu bhaji, korola, dherosh, shak), and dal.
Bhortas and bhajis are sides, not mains; dal goes with rice or bread.

## Rice
"ভাত" is plain steamed rice. "নরমাল/সাধারণ ভাত" means the plain white rice, not the scented (সুগন্ধি/নাজিরশাইল)
one. Polao, khichuri, biryani, kacchi and tehari ARE rice dishes — never offer them as "something without rice",
and never suggest ভাত with them (nobody eats ভাত with a polao). Rice or bread goes with a curry, bhuna, fish or kebab.
when: ভাত, rice, bhat, পোলাও, খিচুড়ি, বিরিয়ানি

## Breakfast (নাস্তা)
Breakfast is bread — paratha, ruti, tandoori ruti, naan — with dal (mug, buter), bhaji, egg (dim bhaji/omelette),
nehari, paya, kolija bhuna, beef bhuna or soup, and tea. Desserts, coffee and juices are not breakfast picks.
when: সকাল, নাস্তা, breakfast, morning, nasta, ভোর

## Afternoon snacks / evening (বিকেলের নাস্তা, সন্ধ্যা)
In many restaurants the evening is for kebabs, grills, chaap, shawarma, tikka with naan or paratha, plus tea.
"বিকেলের নাস্তা" is this snack period — not the lunch rice meal. Use the restaurant's own periods for the times.
when: বিকাল, বিকেল, সন্ধ্যা, evening, afternoon, kabab, kebab, কাবাব, গ্রিল, চাপ, শর্মা

## Paratha and bread
"পরোটা" can mean any paratha on the menu (প্যাঁচ পরোটা, লাচ্ছা, আলু…): when only one is served now, that's the one.
If the guest asks for a kind that isn't on the menu (আলু পরোটা), say so and offer the paratha there is — don't add it
silently. With kebabs and grills, offer paratha or naan.
when: পরোটা, পরাটা, রুটি, নান, paratha, porota, ruti, naan, bread

## Dal
Restaurants often have more than one dal (মুগ, বুটের/ছোলার, মসুর). "ডাল" alone may need a "which one?" with prices.
when: ডাল, dal, daal

## Bhorta and bhaji
"কি কি ভর্তা আছে?" — list the bhortas (mashed) only; bhajis (fried vegetables) are a separate list. "ভর্তা দিন" with
several bhortas → ask which one. Bhorta/bhaji are good add-ons to a rice meal.
when: ভর্তা, ভাজি, ভাজা, bhorta, vorta, bhaji

## Chicken
Guests often ask for "চিকেন ভুনা" or "মুরগির ভুনা". If the menu has no dish by that name, say so and list the
chicken dishes it does have (ঝোল, রোস্ট, ঝাল ফ্রাই, কারি…) — don't offer beef or fish first.
when: চিকেন, মুরগি, মুরগির, chicken, murgi

## Sweets and drinks
Doi (cup/sora/hari), firni, payesh, puding, jorda, halwa are desserts — after the meal. Lassi, borhani and lebu pani
go with a rice meal; tea goes with breakfast and evening snacks. Chilli marks (🌶) on desserts or drinks in some menus
are the restaurant's own badges — don't call a dessert or tea spicy because of them.
when: দই, মিষ্টি, ডেজার্ট, ফিরনি, পুডিং, লাচ্ছি, চা, dessert, sweet, lassi, tea, doi
