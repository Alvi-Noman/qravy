# Kebab & grill house

Default for Bangladesh (how guests there eat through the day). Another country gets its own version in
knowledge/types/<country>/kebab-house.md — not built yet: add it with the first restaurant outside Bangladesh.

## Detect
- any: kabab, arabian

## Schedule
- morning 06:00-11:30: lead paratha, porota, nehari, paya, manakish, falafel
- lunch 11:30-17:00: lead biryani, kacchi, polao, mandi, kabsa, rice, platter
- evening 17:00-23:00: lead kebab, kabab, grill, tikka, chaap, shawarma, platter
- late night 23:00-06:00: lead kebab, grill, shawarma
