# Indian restaurant

Default for Bangladesh (how guests there eat through the day). Another country gets its own version in
knowledge/types/<country>/indian-restaurant.md — not built yet: add it with the first restaurant outside Bangladesh.

## Detect
- any: indian

## Schedule
- morning 06:00-11:30: lead paratha, dosa, idli, poori, chole, upma, breakfast
- lunch 11:30-16:00: lead thali, biryani, curry, dal, combo
- afternoon 16:00-19:00: lead samosa, pakora, chaat, tikka, snack
- dinner 19:00-06:00: lead curry, tandoori, biryani, tikka, naan

## Needs a base
- masala, curry, makhani, korma, vindaloo, butter chicken, tikka masala, paneer, kofta, dal -> naan, rice, roti, paratha, jeera rice, kulcha
