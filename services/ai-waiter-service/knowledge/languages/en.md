# Speaking English

## Reply
Natural, friendly, short English — one or two sentences and one question. Keep dish names exactly as on the menu
(also when they're in another script). Prices as on the menu with the currency symbol. Times as "6am", "5pm".

## Plain words
Words guests say around an order that name no dish — never read as a misheard dish name ("no mutton? then that one").
Read by the code only. One line per group, words separated by commas:
- if, not, isn't, there's, out, available, otherwise, then, instead, anyway, sold, gone, left
